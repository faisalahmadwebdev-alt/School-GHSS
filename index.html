Principal's Message

Welcome to GHSS Jalsai Swabi

Principal, Government Higher Secondary School Jalsai

Assalam-o-Alaikum! It is a matter of great pride for me to welcome you to Government Higher Secondary School Jalsai, Swabi. Our institution has been serving the community of Jalsai and surrounding areas with dedication, providing quality education to students of Class 9 through 12.

We believe in nurturing not just academic excellence but also strong moral character, discipline, and a spirit of service to the nation. Our experienced faculty, well-equipped science and computer labs, library, and expansive playground together create an environment where students can grow intellectually, physically, and spiritually.

I invite parents and students to join hands with us in shaping a brighter future for the youth of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Why Choose GHSS Jalsai?

Quality Education

Comprehensive curriculum aligned with BISE Mardan and KP Textbook Board, taught by experienced and dedicated faculty members.

Modern Science Labs

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories to promote practical, hands-on learning and scientific curiosity.

Computer Lab

Modern computer lab with internet access, preparing our students for the digital challenges of the 21st century.

Sports & Activities

Cricket, football, volleyball, and annual sports gala — we believe in the holistic development of every student.

Well-Stocked Library

A peaceful library with thousands of books, reference materials, and newspapers for students and staff.

Character Building

Emphasis on Islamic values, discipline, and community service — shaping responsible citizens of Pakistan.
