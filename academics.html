Classes 9 to 12 — Matric & Intermediate Programs

Home  ›  Academics

Programs Offered

Class 9 & 10 (Matric)

Two-year Matriculation program following the BISE Mardan / KP Textbook Board curriculum. Students choose between Science and Arts groups after Class 9.

FSc Pre-Medical (11 & 12)

For students aspiring to pursue MBBS, BDS, or allied health sciences. Subjects: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, Urdu, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies.

FSc Pre-Engineering (11 & 12)

For future engineers and technologists. Subjects: Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, English, Urdu, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies.

FA / ICom (11 & 12)

Arts and Commerce groups offering subjects like Civics, Economics, Islamic Studies, History, and Commerce-related subjects.

BISE Mardan Examination Info

Annual Exams

Matric (9th & 10th) and Intermediate (11th & 12th) annual examinations are conducted by BISE Mardan, typically in March–April.

Roll Number Slips

Roll number slips are issued through the school office. Students can also download them from the BISE Mardan website.

Results

Results are announced by BISE Mardan on their official portal. Top position holders are honoured in the school's annual prize distribution.
