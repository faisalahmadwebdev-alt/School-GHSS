Admission Process

Step 1: Registration

Visit the school office or submit the online inquiry form below. Provide basic student information and previous school details.

Step 2: Merit / Interview

Admission to Class 9 is based on the Class 8 result. Admission to Class 11 is based on Matric (Class 10) result as per merit.

Step 3: Document Submission

Submit all required documents to the school office. Verify information and complete the admission form.

Step 4: Confirmation

Receive confirmation from the office. Government schools charge minimal/nominal fees as per KP Government policy.
