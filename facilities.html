Campus Facilities

Modern infrastructure for holistic learning

Home  ›  Facilities

Science Laboratories

Separate, well-equipped laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. Students perform practical experiments aligned with the BISE Mardan curriculum.

Computer Lab

Modern computer lab with internet connectivity, enabling students to gain essential IT skills and explore the digital world under teacher supervision.

Library

A well-stocked library containing textbooks, reference books, Islamic literature, and newspapers. A quiet reading room is available for students.

Playground & Sports

A large playground for cricket, football, and volleyball. Annual sports gala and inter-class competitions are held regularly.

Prayer Area

A clean prayer area for students and staff to offer prayers, promoting spiritual well-being alongside academic growth.

Green Campus

Beautifully maintained hedges, trees, and green spaces — providing a healthy and pleasant learning environment.

Classrooms

Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms with proper furniture and whiteboards for effective teaching and learning.

Safe Environment

A disciplined, secure campus with strict adherence to school rules and a dedicated staff ensuring student safety.
